why this offer

Private reformer Pilates isn’t about intensity — it’s about intention. On the reformer, every spring, breath, and alignment cue is tailored to you — so you leave stronger, more centered, and more connected to how your body moves.

faq

I’ve never done reformer — is this offer okay for first-timers?

Yes. Your Private Pilates Reformer session is paced for you. Your instructor will introduce the reformer, springs, and foundational movements so you feel supported from the first session.

What should I wear to my Private Reformer session?

Comfortable, fitted athletic wear you can move in. Grip socks are helpful on the reformer carriage. Arrive a few minutes early so you can settle in.

What’s the cancellation policy for this offer?

Please contact us at least 24 hours before your private reformer session if you need to cancel or reschedule.

How is Private Reformer different from barre?

Private reformer is one-on-one on the Pilates reformer machine — individualized cues, spring resistance, and personal focus for 60 minutes. Barre at barrelosophy is a separate small-group studio class format.

When can I book this offer?

Private reformer is offered Tue / Thu / Sun (per the homepage). Book online or start from the Private Reformer service page.
